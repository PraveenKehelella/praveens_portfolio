01 / About
A generalist who ships.

Software developer with eight years spanning robotics, machine vision, full-stack, and agentic AI / RAG. I build LLM-powered software that holds up in production — including live products on OpenAI models with retrieval pipelines — and I work comfortably from high-level architecture down to low-level design.

Right now I lead agentic AI development for Omnigate AI AB (Sweden), including production work in healthcare IT. Before that I spent nearly four years at Rabot Inc. in Texas on edge AI, MLOps, and a video-insight product that helped the company win customers.

I like problems with a hardware constraint, a data constraint, or both — the kind where the interesting work happens at the boundary between systems.
